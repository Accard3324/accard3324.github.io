🚀 What This Tool Does

This software helps you list products on Shopee faster and safer. It works in two stages:

  • Stage 1 (AI Prepares): AI generates product titles, descriptions, and keywords based on your input. You can review and edit everything before it goes live.
  • Stage 2 (Deterministic Automation): Once you approve the content, the software automatically fills in the Shopee seller dashboard — no manual copy-pasting, no errors.

You stay in control: AI suggests, you approve, the robot executes.

✨ Features

🧠 AI Content Generation — Get smart suggestions for titles, descriptions, and keywords.
✅ Review Before Listing — See what will be posted. Edit or reject before automation runs.
🤖 Deterministic Automation — The bot follows exact steps. No randomness, no guesswork.
🪟 Windows Compatible — Runs on Windows 10 and 11. No Linux or Mac support.
🔒 Open Source — Code is fully visible. No hidden data collection.
⚡ Fast Batch Listing — List multiple products in one session.

🔧 First-Time Setup

  1. Open the app. You'll see a welcome screen.
  2. Log in to your Shopee seller account when prompted (your credentials stay private on your computer).
  3. Enter product details or upload a spreadsheet with your product data.
  4. Click "Generate AI Content" — review and edit the suggestions.
  5. Click "Start Listing" — the automation will fill in the Shopee form for you.
💡 Tip: Start with one product to test. The automation mimics human typing — do not move your mouse or press keys while it runs.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is this safe to use?

Yes. The software is open-source, meaning anyone can inspect the code. It does not send your data anywhere except to Shopee as part of the listing process.

Do I need programming skills?

No. The entire process is point-and-click. Just download, install, and follow the on-screen instructions.

Can I edit the AI content?

Absolutely. The AI is a helper — you have the final say. You can change any text before the automation runs.

Will it work on Mac or Linux?

No. This version is built for Windows only. Mac/Linux users may need to run it in a virtual machine.

📋 Troubleshooting

  • App won't start: Make sure you have Windows 10/11 and the latest version of Chrome or Edge.
  • Automation stops mid-way: Close other browser windows. Ensure your internet is stable. Restart the app.
  • Antivirus warning: This is normal for automation tools. Add the app to your antivirus exclusions if needed.
